# Placement Resources

## 1. Common Placement Eligibility Criteria

Placement eligibility requirements vary by college, university, and recruiting company. Students should always check the official eligibility criteria mentioned in the company's job description or placement notice before applying. The following are common criteria seen in campus placement policies and recruiter requirements.

### 1.1 Minimum CGPA / Academic Percentage

* Many colleges and companies set a minimum academic requirement, usually expressed as a **CGPA or percentage**.
* There is no single CGPA requirement that applies to every company. The required threshold may vary by recruiter, role, course, and institution.
* For example, some institutional placement policies use thresholds around **6.0–6.75 CGPA**, while some companies specify a **60% aggregate or equivalent CGPA** across academic qualifications.
* Students should maintain consistent academic performance throughout their degree and verify whether the company considers Class 10, Class 12, diploma, graduation, or postgraduate marks.

### 1.2 Backlogs / Arrears

* Companies and institutions commonly place restrictions on **active or pending backlogs**.
* Some placement policies require students to have **no active backlogs** when participating in recruitment.
* However, requirements can differ. For example, a current TCS hiring program allows candidates with up to one backlog during the selection process, provided the course is completed without backlogs by the time of joining.
* Students should therefore check whether a company permits:

  * No backlogs only
  * A limited number of backlogs
  * Past/cleared backlogs
  * Backlogs only until a specified stage of the recruitment process

### 1.3 Attendance Requirements

* Colleges may require students to maintain a minimum attendance percentage to participate in placement activities.
* A **75% attendance requirement** is common in several institutional placement policies, although the actual requirement varies by institution.
* Some institutions also require attendance in placement-specific activities such as aptitude training, workshops, mock interviews, or pre-placement sessions. For example, CMR University requires 75% attendance in pre-placement training classes.
* Students should check both their **academic attendance requirement** and any separate **placement-training attendance requirement**.

### 1.4 Course, Graduation, and Academic Background

* Eligibility may depend on the student's **degree, specialization, graduation year, and academic background**.
* Companies may specify particular eligible programs such as B.E./B.Tech., BCA, MCA, M.Sc., or other relevant degrees.
* Recruiters may also specify the required graduation batch or passing year. For example, current TCS hiring programs specify eligible degree programs and particular graduating batches.
* Students should carefully check whether their branch or specialization is included in the job description.

### 1.5 Company-Specific Criteria

* Meeting the college's general placement eligibility does **not automatically make a student eligible for every company**.
* Recruiters can define additional requirements based on the role and hiring process. These may include:

  * Minimum academic percentage/CGPA
  * Specific degree or branch
  * Graduation year
  * Number of permitted backlogs
  * Relevant technical skills
  * Required certifications or experience
  * Restrictions on education gaps or extended education
* For example, TCS recruitment pages specify academic aggregate, backlog, qualification, and education-duration requirements for individual hiring programs.
* Therefore, students should treat the **official company job description or placement notice as the final source of eligibility requirements** for a particular recruitment drive.

### 1.6 Education Gaps and Extended Education

* Some recruiters place conditions on **gaps in education or extended duration of study**.
* These requirements are not universal and should be checked for each recruitment drive.
* For example, TCS states specific conditions regarding declared education gaps and extended education in some current hiring programs.

### 1.7 Other Institutional Requirements

Depending on the college or university, students may also need to:

* Register with the Training and Placement (T&P) Cell.
* Complete mandatory placement-training programs.
* Attend pre-placement talks and recruitment activities.
* Maintain appropriate conduct and comply with placement-cell rules.
* Submit accurate academic and personal information.
* Provide required documents such as resumes, mark sheets, certificates, or identification documents.

For example, institutional policies from Adichunchanagiri University and Noida International University include registration/training, attendance, documentation, and conduct-related requirements.

### Quick Checklist for Students

Before applying to a placement drive, students should verify:

* [ ] Required minimum CGPA/percentage
* [ ] Class 10 and Class 12 requirements, if applicable
* [ ] Degree/branch eligibility
* [ ] Graduation year or batch
* [ ] Active/past backlog requirements
* [ ] Attendance requirements
* [ ] Education-gap or extended-education rules
* [ ] Required skills or certifications
* [ ] Placement-cell registration requirements
* [ ] Required documents
* [ ] Any additional company-specific conditions

> **Important:** Placement eligibility criteria are not universal. The criteria published by the recruiting company for a particular job/drive and the student's institution should be treated as the authoritative requirements for that recruitment process.
